# First aid, shelter and environmental hazards

## First aid

Use current accredited first-aid training and locally applicable guidance. If a person is seriously injured or not breathing normally, call emergency services as soon as possible and follow dispatcher instructions. Maintain an accessible first-aid kit and list any special medical equipment that depends on electricity.

## Smoke, fire and carbon monoxide

If you detect smoke, fire or suspected toxic gas, get to safety and follow evacuation instructions. Certified smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms are needed; experimental sensors and dashboards must not replace them. A DIY measurement can be inaccurate, drift over time or fail when power is disrupted.

## Shelter

Avoid unstable structures, exposed wiring, floodwater and contaminated ground. Prepare weather-appropriate clothing, blankets, ventilation and an evacuation bag. Never use unflued outdoor gas appliances, charcoal grills or engines indoors for cooking or heating.

## Document conditions

Use field notes to record observations and the time. Clearly distinguish your observations from professional instructions and unverified reports. Keep paper copies of critical procedures in case the device is unavailable.
