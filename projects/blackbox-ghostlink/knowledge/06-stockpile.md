# Supplies and equipment continuity

## Inventory rules

For each supply, record actual quantity, units, minimum reserve, expiry, storage location and status. Do not insert demo inventory into a live emergency system. A zero quantity or missing record is not proof that a supply does not physically exist: audit it before making decisions.

## Periodic checks

Inspect batteries, leaks, seals, first-aid expiry dates, light output, charging leads, portable radio reception, printed documents and the condition of stored water. Record test date, result, next action and a realistic replenishment threshold.

## Planning basics

Measure consumption over time rather than assuming fixed daily needs. Account for people, pets, weather, medical requirements, strenuous activity and unexpected delays. Emergency guidance from your local authority may establish different storage requirements.
