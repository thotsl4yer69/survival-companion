# Navigation with and without a network

## Offline map preparation

Download legally usable geographic data *before* internet access is lost. BLACKBOX can store waypoints and export GeoJSON or GPX, but it does not ship with an offline terrain basemap or an attached GPS receiver. A coordinate entry alone does not verify a physical location.

## Cross-check

Use a paper map, compass, marked route and known landmarks. A standalone GNSS receiver can work without internet, but accurate fixes depend on satellite visibility and device power. Check datum, coordinate order, elevation and map currency.

## Decision-making

Follow official closure and evacuation information, avoid entering floodwaters, and do not trust an automatically calculated route as an indication that it is passable. Share intended route and check-in arrangements with trusted contacts when possible.
