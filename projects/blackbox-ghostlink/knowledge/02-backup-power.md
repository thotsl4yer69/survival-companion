# Backup power and electrical safety

## Start with measured loads

Calculate the power requirement of each essential device: watts multiplied by hours of daily use gives watt-hours. Account for conversion losses, cold or hot battery conditions, charger inefficiency and reserve capacity. Confirm the actual input power and starting current of refrigerators, compressors and communications equipment.

## Prioritise

1. Life-support and medical equipment, with professionally recommended backup arrangements.
2. Communications, certified alarms and essential lighting.
3. Safe storage of medications and perishable food where applicable.
4. Convenience devices only after essentials have adequate reserve.

## Safety boundaries

Never operate petrol, diesel, LPG or other combustion generators inside a building, garage or enclosed area. Carbon monoxide can kill without warning. Never backfeed household wiring through an ordinary outlet. Use approved transfer equipment and qualified installers for mains-connected systems. Do not improvise lithium battery packs, charging circuits, high-current wiring or fusing.

## Device continuity

For a Jetson or Raspberry Pi, use stable regulated power and monitor both input voltage and filesystem health. Perform clean shutdowns and periodically test restoring a database backup. An online dashboard does not mean the battery runtime has been verified.
